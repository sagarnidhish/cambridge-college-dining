# Dining directory roadmap

This file records ideas that are intentionally outside the current public-data release. They should not be inferred from, or mixed into, the evidence used by the meal-availability list.

## Café and bar directory

- Add venue-level records for cafés, bars, snack counters, and vacation alternatives.
- Record opening hours, audience, payment, age restrictions, accessibility, exact Maps location, and the source date separately from dining-hall meals.
- Do not treat a public café/bar as proof that the college dining hall is open or accessible.

## Community discussion

- Consider a moderated, college-and-date-specific comment layer only after defining moderation, reporting, retention, and anti-abuse rules.
- Keep community reports visibly separate from official evidence; a comment may flag a change but must not silently override an official status.
- Avoid importing or republishing third-party discussion content without permission and clear attribution.

## Cambridge-login guest requests

- Explore University of Cambridge single sign-on only with an approved identity integration and a minimal-data privacy review.
- A request workflow would need consent, expiry, rate limits, blocking/reporting, audit trails, and a clear statement that a request is not a confirmed booking or right of entry.
- Do not expose student contact details publicly or scrape authenticated college systems.
